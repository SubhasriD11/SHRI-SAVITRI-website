## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end Indo-Western couture tailored for modern Gen-Z cultural sensibilities. The visual language balances editorial minimalism with heritage artisan craft: uncluttered expanses of warm ivory and sandstone, precise micro-details, architectural silhouettes, and editorial typography that evokes the gravitas of print fashion journalism. 

The aesthetic style is **Editorial High-Fashion Minimalism with Tactile Resonance**:
- Spacious, deliberate negative space mirroring contemporary gallery curation.
- Muted organic tones framing garments rather than competing with them.
- Subdued hairline borders and micro-accents of burnished antique gold that honor South Asian metallic weaving (Zari) without visual gaudiness.
- High-contrast visual scale where expansive imagery and striking editorial headers command the viewport.

## Colors

The color palette is grounded in natural textiles, stone courtyards, and mineral pigments:

- **Primary (`#1A1615`) - Deep Espresso Charcoal:** Replaces stark black for a richer, softer editorial contrast across typographic headlines, high-priority buttons, and dominant silhouettes.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`) - Burnished Antique Gold:** A muted, non-reflective metallic tone used for heritage tags, craftsmanship markers, active micro-states, and subtle decorative borders.
- **Tertiary (`#C85A32`) - Terracotta Vermillion:** A spirited mineral earth accent referencing sindoor and baked clay, reserved for seasonal drops, limited-edition pills, and alert states.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5`) - Warm Ivory:** The overarching light canvas, supplemented by sandstone `#F5F0EA` for container surfaces and `#EAE4DC` for hairline boundaries.
- **Accent Slate (`#687259`) - Muted Olive Sage:** An organic foil for drape styles, textile origin disclosures, and secondary chips.

Strict contrast guidelines must be maintained: pure black (`#000000`) and optical pure white (`#FFFFFF`) are prohibited to protect the organic, tactile atmosphere of the surfaces.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between traditional regal posture and contemporary international layout.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in `Playfair Display` with natural proportional tracking and generous vertical line height, bringing the authority of high-fashion magazines to Indo-Western collections.
- **Body & Micro-Copy:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean, unadorned legibility across fabric compositions, garment dimensions, and checkout workflows.
- **Labels, Badges, & Metadata:** Formatted in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury house cataloging systems.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a structured 12-column fluid grid on desktop, scaling to a 6-column system on tablet and a 2-column or 4-column framework on mobile devices.

- **Editorial Rhythm:** Large margins (`3rem`) create framing around lookbooks and product carousels, allowing photography to occupy center-stage without edge tension.
- **Mobile Adaptations:** The horizontal margin drops to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), maintaining breathing room while prioritizing full-bleed lookbook visuals.
- **Asymmetrical Offsets:** Editorial feature layouts may employ intentional 1-column offsets to break standard e-commerce uniformity and present drape details with editorial pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on structural surface layering and low-contrast borders:

- **Surface Layering:** The base background is Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`). Cards, elevated drawers, and contextual panels shift to Sandstone (`#F5F0EA`) to communicate lift without physical weight.
- **Hairline Outlines:** Overlays, cards, and modal dividers employ a razor-thin 1px border in `#EAE4DC` (optical tone: muted sandstone beige).
- **Floating Controls:** Floating navigation bars and interactive quick-action trays utilize semi-translucent backdrops (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.82)`) with a `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), outlined by a `1px` stroke of `#EAE4DC`.
- **Soft Ambient Focus:** When popovers or quick-buy drawers engage, depth is marked by an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural geometry with targeted softness:

- **Architectural Framing:** Image containers, product tiles, and structural panels utilize crisp `0.25rem` (`roundedness: 1`) or razor-sharp corners to evoke print editorials and lookbook frames.
- **Interactive Pill Contrast:** While cards and images maintain disciplined geometry, interactive controls (category filters, craft pills, size selectors) adopt full-rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to create a tactile, touch-friendly touchpoint for younger mobile users.
- **Hairline Consistency:** All borders and divider treatments remain strictly fixed at 1px thickness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Charcoal (`#1A1615`) fill with Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`) text. Rectangular with soft `0.25rem` corners, uppercase `label-md` typography, and generous vertical padding (`0.875rem` vertical, `2rem` horizontal).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a `1px` border of `#1A1615` and Deep Charcoal text. On hover, transitions smoothly to Sandstone (`#F5F0EA`).
- **Tertiary / Gold Micro-Action:** Text-only button styled in Burnished Antique Gold (`#C5A059`) with an understated underline offset by `4px`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Sleek full-pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- Inactive state: Sandstone background (`#F5F0EA`), hairline border (`#EAE4DC`), and Deep Charcoal body text.
- Active state: Deep Charcoal (`#1A1615`) fill with Ivory text, accompanied by an optional micro-dot indicator in Antique Gold (`#C5A059`).

### Craftsmanship & Drape Style Tags
- Special luxury metadata tags indicating techniques like *Aari Embroidery*, *Chanderi Silk*, or *Pre-Draped Sari*.
- Styled with an ivory background, a `1px` border of Burnished Antique Gold (`#C5A059`), and `label-sm` gold typography.

### Product Cards
- Borderless frame with a 4:5 or 3:4 high-fashion aspect ratio.
- Surrounded by an optional hairline border (`#EAE4DC`) on hover.
- Information layout: Left-aligned title in `headline-sm`, subtitle/fabric in `body-sm` neutral, and right-aligned price in `body-md` Charcoal. No aggressive sale badges; limited runs are tagged with a minimal Terracotta (`#C85A32`) pill badge positioned on the top left.

### Floating Navigation Bar
- Floats detached from the top viewport edge with `1.5rem` desktop margin and `1rem` mobile margin.
- Sandstone/Ivory frosted glass treatment (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)` with `blur(16px)`).
- Centered brand logotype with delicate 18px stroke icons for search, wishlist, and bag.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat sandstone-tinted input fields (`#F5F0EA`) with a bottom-only or subtle hairline border (`#EAE4DC`).
- Active focus state transitions the border to Deep Charcoal (`#1A1615`) without glowing halos.
- Floating label transitioning smoothly from `body-md` to uppercase `label-sm`.
